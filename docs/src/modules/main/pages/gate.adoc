`SecurityGate` is the scanner-agnostic, runtime-checkable protocol for
`scan(text, *, content_type) -> GateVerdict`. `Runner` supports its concrete
`SimpleGate` and `CascadeGate` implementations. A simple gate evaluates one ordered scanner list. A cascade
runs ordered stages, each with its own configured scanners and decision rule.
Absent root `type` or `type: simple` keeps existing simple-gate behavior.

== Simple gate: findings and scores

A scanner emits a `ScanOutcome`: risk score, rationale, and optional match
spans. The gate adds the scanner ID, display name, severity, and action to make
a `Finding`.

Only positive-risk findings with action `BLOCK` contribute to the aggregate. Their input to
the configured score aggregator is:

[source,text]
----
weighted score = finding.risk_score × finding.severity.weight
----

The entry is safe when there are no positive-risk blocking findings or when the aggregate is
strictly below `threshold`. `WARN` and `PASS` findings are retained for review
but do not contribute to the aggregate.

== Simple gate: content-type routing

Scanners may restrict their accepted `content_types`. Before calling a scanner,
the gate compares the entry's type with that restriction. A non-matching scanner
is recorded as skipped with its reason; it does not produce a finding or affect
the aggregate.

== Simple gate: fail-fast behavior

When `fail_fast` is enabled, the gate stops after the first blocking finding
whose risk score is not within `1e-9` of zero and whose severity-weighted score
reaches the configured threshold. Near-zero positive scores still contribute to
the aggregate. With fail-fast disabled,
the gate runs every compatible scanner and returns all findings.

== Simple gate: scanner errors

`on_scanner_error` determines how scanner exceptions affect the result:

|===
|Policy |Behavior

|`fail` |Re-raises the original error.
|`block` |Continues according to fail-fast settings and forces an unsafe verdict.
|`skip` |Records a skipped scanner with an execution-failure reason.
|===

The output `GateVerdict` includes `is_safe`, aggregate `score`, rationale,
findings, and skipped scanners. See
xref:components/score-aggregators.adoc[Score aggregators] for the available
combination strategies.

== Cascade stages

Each stage runs its scanners in configured order. All compatible scanners in a
reached stage run before its decision is evaluated, unless an operational
scanner failure stops execution immediately. Unlike a simple-gate skip, a
cascade scanner whose `content_types` reject the entry is `unsupported_content`
and is an operational fault: `fail` raises it, while `block` returns an unsafe
operational verdict. It stops the cascade; remaining scanners in that stage
do not run and later stages have `result: not_reached`. A terminal allow or block
prevents later stages from running. Every stage is numerical; the final stage
allows every score not blocked. See xref:configuration.adoc[Flat cascade
configuration] for the exact YAML schema. Every nonfinal stage requires an
explicit `defer_above` smaller than its resolved threshold. The final stage
forbids `defer_above`, including in a single-stage cascade.

=== Stages and evidence

A stage uses only its own completed raw scanner outcomes. Earlier outcomes and
aggregate scores are never selected or rescored. Eligible scores
are positive own BLOCK findings weighted by configured severity and passed
unchanged to the configured aggregator. No eligible evidence means score `0`
without an aggregator call. A block requires aggregate `score > threshold`;
equality does not block. Stage `threshold` overrides the optional root default.

For instance, an earlier deferred score `0.60` does not affect a final stage
with threshold `0.55` and own score `0.0`: the final stage allows. Earlier
findings remain visible in output. Max aggregation does not prove scanner
independence or probability calibration.

=== Score-only stage decisions

Blocking is evaluated first. On a nonfinal stage, required `defer_above` defers
scores strictly above it; equality is eligible for allow. Missing nonfinal
bands and any final-stage band are configuration errors. Otherwise the stage
allows, including at score zero or equality with `defer_above`. Content type
does not add an early-allow condition; scanner eligibility remains separate.

The final stage allows every unblocked score and must not configure a defer band.
Required scanner failures and known-type
mismatches still fail or block before any stage decision can allow.

=== Cascade operational errors and trace

The gate-level `on_scanner_error` is `fail` by default or `block`. `fail`
preserves the original scanner/transport error where possible. `block` returns
an unsafe operational verdict with a bounded failure category in `rationale`;
it does not fabricate a threat finding. The entry score `1.0` is a fail-closed
sentinel, not model risk, and the blocking stage omits its score. Remaining
scanners in that stage do not run; later stages have `result: not_reached`.

Public `StageResult` summaries expose only `id`, `result`, and optional `score`.
Normal reached stages include their diagnostic score; unreached stages omit it.
`Finding.stage` identifies the stage that executed that scanner. All completed
findings remain in one flat list in execution order, including deferred stages,
without duplicating or rescoring earlier findings. Simple gates serialize `stages: null`
and omit unset finding stage fields. Top-level result data can
still contain text and finding rationale.
